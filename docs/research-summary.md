# Governed Decision Intelligence: findings, use and next steps

Reviewed 29 October 2026. This account synthesizes the linked records at source revision `6915e5ab06d8346dd9ee359236430b273496dff1`. It adds no validation claim, release decision or change to the underlying method.

Status: Specification & reference implementation. Evidence: Internal implementation checks.

## Question and contribution

An institution acts on an AI-informed recommendation. What record would let someone reconstruct its evidence, authority and obligations?

A decision-record specification, gate classifier and signed-receipt demonstration. Internal checks establish limited properties of the supplied records and fixtures.

## Findings to date

Schema and negative-fixture checks examine required fields and selected relationships. Classifier tests exercise supplied routing rules. Hash, signature and chain checks examine defined integrity properties of demonstration records.

Mark Julius Banasihan contributed the original GDI driver to <a href="https://github.com/ScopeBlind/agent-governance-testvectors/pull/7">ScopeBlind’s test-vector suite</a>. Maintainer <a href="https://github.com/ScopeBlind/agent-governance-testvectors/pull/7#issuecomment-6390516611">@tomjwxf reviewed and revised the implementation</a>, preserving the four original commits. PR #7 remains open and unmerged as checked on 29 October 2026. The maintainer’s reported upstream results remain separate from GDI’s three local fixtures.

## How to use the work

Specify decision evidence, record alternatives and authority, and test whether required information survives into an inspectable record.

## Current status and why

GDI remains an open specification and reference implementation. Its record format and implementation checks are available for inspection; an institutional-effectiveness study has not been established by these checks.

## Limits and next evidence

A valid or signed record does not establish its factual truth, reviewer comprehension, appropriate calibration, lawful action or effective human control. Receipt interoperability is narrower than validation of the research program.

Define a prospective comparison of record reconstruction or operational intervention, with independent assessment and evidence suited to the exact benefit claimed.
